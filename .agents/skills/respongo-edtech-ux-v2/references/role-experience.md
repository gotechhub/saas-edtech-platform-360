# Role experience

## Learner

Lead with one continue action, required deadlines, personal journey, recommendations, live sessions, progress, certificates, achievements, and community. Keep choices small and language encouraging.

## Tenant admin

Lead with today's work queue, compliance risk, adoption, content readiness, assignments, integration health, support, and quick actions. Use readable tables, saved filters, previews, bulk actions, and clear completion feedback.

## Instructor

Lead with today's sessions, grading queue, unanswered questions, content health, cohort progress, rubrics, and feedback drafts.

## Line manager

Lead with team risk, overdue requirements, approvals, skills gaps, development suggestions, and scoped team detail. Never reveal data outside direct team scope.

## Platform admin

Lead with portal fleet health, trials, support SLA, failed jobs, quotas, security events, releases, and the sector-aware portal creation workflow. Customer learning detail requires an audited support scope.
