# Product context

Respongo Learning is a multi-tenant SaaS platform combining LMS, LXP, gamification, learning operations, support, reporting, and lightweight authoring. Oguz Law Academy is the first portal and legal is the first industry pack; the platform must remain ready for hospitality and other industries.

The product replaces fragmented HR and L&D tools with one guided workspace. It must help users decide what to do next, help administrators find operational risk quickly, and help platform operators provision and support many portals safely.

Primary product routes use `/{industry}/{tenant}`. Turkish is the primary language and English readiness is required. Web, mobile web, and Expo use the same concepts and semantic tokens.
